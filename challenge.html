The Milk Challenge

Official Rules, Regulations & Disqualification Terms
Section 1 •

Specifications

  • Dairy Standard: Whole milk only. No substitutes.
  • Vessel Requirements: Clear container only (1.89L / Half Gallon minimum).
  • Pre-Approved Flavors: Chocolate, strawberry, or caramel syrup are permitted.
  • Custom Flavors: Any other flavors must be submitted to and evaluated by the official group chat prior to kickoff.
Section 2 •

Timing & Vessels

  • Time Limit: Strictly 15 minutes on the clock.
  • Initial State: All milk must start fully contained inside the half-gallon jug before starting the timer.
  • Alternative Vessels: If using a cup, bowl, or secondary vessel, the timer MUST be running before you begin pouring.
Disqualification Penalty

If you pour milk into another vessel AND fail to finish the entire half gallon, your entry will be automatically DQ'd and you will receive the last pick in the draft.

Section 3 •

Incompletion Protocol

If you fail to finish within the allotted 15 minutes, you must complete the following steps immediately:

  1. Place your bottle on a flat surface and take a photo showing the clear water/liquid level.
  2. Measure remaining milk from the bottom of the jug to the liquid line.
  3. Submit photographic evidence of the physical measurement to the group chat.
Section 4 •

Probation & Removal

  • Opt-Out Status: If you choose not to compete, you will automatically be placed on Probation.
  • Removal Rule: Placing on probation 2 years in a row results in immediate removal/kick from the group.
