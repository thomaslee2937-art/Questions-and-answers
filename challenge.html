Milk Challenge

Official Competition Guidelines & Bylaws

Equipment & Ingredients

  • Dairy Standard: Whole milk only.
  • Vessel Requirements: Must be served in a clear container holding a 1.89L minimum capacity (half gallon).
  • Flavoring Regulations: Chocolate, strawberry, and caramel syrup are strictly allowed. Any alternative flavors must be evaluated and approved by the group chat prior to start time.

Timing & Pouring Rules

  • Time Limit: Competitors have exactly 15 minutes to finish.
  • Initial Setup: All liquid must reside inside the primary half-gallon jug before starting the timer.
  • Alternative Vessels: If using a secondary cup, bowl, or vessel, the official timer MUST be running before any pouring begins.

Measurement & Penalties

  • Incomplete Finish Protocol: If you do not finish within the 15-minute limit, you must place your bottle on a level, flat surface to display the remaining water/milk line.
  • Photo Evidence: A photograph measuring the distance from the bottom of the jug to the liquid level line is required for official verification.
  • Disqualification Penalty: If you pour milk into an auxiliary vessel AND fail to finish the entire half gallon, your entry is automatically DQ'd and you will receive last pick.
  • Probationary Clause: Opting out of competing places you on active probation. Failing to compete for 2 consecutive years results in removal from the league.
